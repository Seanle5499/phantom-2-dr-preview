FAQ

I already have hip pain from carrying. Will Phantom actually help?
Phantom 2.0 is engineered to reduce the mechanical loads that cause CCW-related hip discomfort — the PU foam back spreads pressure off the nerve, and dual clips cut the lateral load in half. We can't promise it'll undo damage you already have (see a doctor about that), but thousands of carriers have switched to Phantom to keep carrying without adding to the problem. And you have 30 days risk-free to find out.
What carry positions does it work with?
10 o'clock to 5 o'clock — appendix, strong-side, kidney. Appendix carry performs best for fast access.
What belt size?
Fits belts up to 1.5". We recommend a 1.5" gun belt for best retention.

No — Phantom 2.0 is non-light-bearing only.

Yes — compatible with top-mounted optics.

No — Phantom 2.0 currently works with flush-fitting barrels only.
