**Q1: What carry positions is the Phantom 2.0 compatible with?**

**Q7: What guns does it fit?**
```
The Phantom 2.0 is a universal-fit IWB holster designed to work with most compact and sub-compact handguns. The PU leather foam molds to your specific firearm's shape within 1–2 days of carry, giving you a custom feel without a model-specific holster. If you're unsure about fitment, email us at support@southedc.store — we'll confirm before you order.
```

**Q8: Will retention loosen up over time?**
```
The Phantom 2.0 uses fixed steel-riveted retention — no adjustment screws, no grommets. It holds a consistent medium draw for years. Your firearm clicks in and stays secure whether it's day one or year three.
```

**Q9: How long does it take to break in?**
```
There's no painful break-in period. The PU leather foam is comfortable from the first wear and conforms to your exact gun shape within 1–2 days. By day two, it feels like it was built for your specific firearm.
```

**Q10: Can I return the Phantom 2.0 if it doesn't meet my expectations?**
```
Yes — we offer a 30-day risk-free return. If you're not completely satisfied, contact our team within 30 days of receiving your order. We'll process a full refund of the product price once we receive it in original condition. Shipping costs are non-refundable.
```

**Q11: Will it scratch my gun?**
```
No. Unlike hard Kydex shells that scrape your slide and wear down the finish over daily holster-and-draw cycles, the Phantom 2.0's PU leather cradles your firearm. Your gun's finish stays clean.
```

**Q12: What about sweat and moisture?**
```
The PU leather back panel acts as a built-in sweat guard — it wraps the grip and blocks moisture from reaching the firearm. Your gun doesn't rust from body sweat, and you don't get the skin irritation that comes from bare metal against damp skin. No dripping gun when you get home after a Florida summer day.
```
