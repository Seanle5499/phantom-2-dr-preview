Does it print under thin clothing?
No. The holster pocket is positioned low on the hip, drawing the handgun flat against your body. It is completely invisible even under thin sweatpants, athletic wear, or gym shorts.

The heavy compression fabric wraps securely around the firearm, and the dual-layered holster pockets provide thick fabric coverage over the trigger guard. However, we always recommend carrying with standard manual safety protocols active on your firearm.
