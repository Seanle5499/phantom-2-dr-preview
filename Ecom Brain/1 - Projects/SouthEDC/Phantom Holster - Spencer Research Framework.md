## Research

### Fundamental

| Component | What is it |
|---|---|
| Core Avatar / Desire | Daily concealed carrier who wants to feel protected without looking armed, printing under clothes, getting pinched, or fighting the holster while seated/driving. |
| Mechanism | Patented breakout channel + faux-leather back panel + dual reinforced steel clips + steel-riveted fixed retention. Product page says it lets you draw "with the flick of the wrist" and supports concealment/comfort. |
| Awareness Level | Problem-aware to solution-aware. They already know IWB holsters can print, pinch, loosen, or feel bulky. They need to believe this mechanism solves those exact daily carry failures. |

### Competitor

| Who I'm up against | How I'm different | Problem it can solve |
|---|---|---|
| Rigid Kydex-style IWB holsters | Softer back panel + concealment-focused fixed retention instead of bulky adjustable screws. | Reduces pinch/bulk while carrying inside waistband all day. |
| Cheap generic concealed-carry holsters | Clear mechanism, steel clips/rivets, lifetime warranty, 30-day guarantee, CCW instructor proof. | Reduces fear of flimsy clips, unsafe retention, or wasted money. |
| Hybrid comfort holsters | Breakout channel gives a concrete draw mechanism, not just "comfortable carry" copy. | Lets the ad sell comfort plus fast access, not comfort alone. |
| Open carry / OWB alternatives | Built for invisible IWB carry under normal clothes. | Solves "I want protection without broadcasting that I carry." |

### The Subavatar

| Component | What is it |
|---|---|
| Subavatar #1 | The everyday CCW carrier in normal clothes: T-shirt, jeans, driving, sitting, running errands. |
| Angle (the one reason to buy) | "Carry all day without printing or pinching, while still getting fast access when it matters." |
| Authority | Product-page proof: "4.7/5 | 368 reviews", CCW Instructor endorsement, handcrafted in USA, lifetime warranty, steel clips/rivets. Use these as trust builders, not fake hype. |
| Valence & Intensity | High intensity. The fear is visible printing, discomfort, slow access, or the holster failing in a defensive moment. The desired state is quiet confidence: protected, invisible, comfortable. |

## Above-the-Fold Matching Rules

If ad angle is no-print carry:
- Hero headline: no printing / undetectable carry.
- Hero visual: T-shirt concealment proof.
- Proof: review count + guarantee near CTA.

If ad angle is comfort while seated:
- Hero headline: comfort while sitting/driving.
- Hero visual: seated driver or desk chair.
- Proof: faux-leather back panel + testimonial.

If ad angle is fast access:
- Hero headline: breakout channel draw access.
- Hero visual: mechanism demo.
- Proof: steel-riveted retention + CCW instructor endorsement.

## Product-Page Claims To Use Carefully

| Claim | Source snippet | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Concealment promise | "Forget you're carrying and remain undetectable" | Main no-print angle. |
| Comfort promise | "All-Day Comfort Without the Pinch" | Sit/drive comfort angle. |
| Mechanism | "Patented Breakout Channel Design" | Authority/mechanism angle. |
| Draw mechanism | "draw instantly with the flick of the wrist" | Fast-access angle. Avoid overpromising beyond product page. |
| Offer | "SAVE 50%", "BUY ONE GET ONE FREE!", "Free Mag Holster Upgrade" | Use as simple offer stack after angle is clear. |
| Risk reversal | 30-day money-back guarantee, lifetime warranty | Lower purchase anxiety. |

## First 5 Ads To Make

1. **No-Print T-Shirt Demo**
   - Format: native iPhone demo.
   - Hook: "This is what concealed carry should look like under a T-shirt. Nothing."
   - Show: side profile, movement, bend/sit.

2. **Driving Comfort Test**
   - Format: UGC problem/solution.
   - Hook: "Most IWB holsters remind you they're there the second you sit down."
   - Show: car seat, seatbelt, no pinch.

3. **Breakout Channel Mechanism**
   - Format: logical authority demo.
   - Hook: "The part of this holster that most people miss is the draw path."
   - Show: close-up mechanism, wrist flick, retention.

4. **Kydex Bulk Contrast**
   - Format: comparison.
   - Hook: "Adjustability is nice until it creates the print you're trying to hide."
   - Show: profile comparison, waistband view.

5. **Instructor Proof + Guarantee**
   - Format: proof-led static or video.
   - Hook: "A concealed-carry instructor's holster test: comfort, concealment, access."
   - Show: instructor-style table demo + warranty/guarantee.
