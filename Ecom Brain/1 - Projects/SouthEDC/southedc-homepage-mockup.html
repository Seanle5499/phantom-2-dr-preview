Veteran-Owned · Family Built

Started By A Vet. Built Around One Question.

In 2015, Kevin moved his family to Chicago, where protecting them stopped being theoretical. The holsters on the market either dug into his side all day, or slowed his draw when it mattered. So he built his own — and invented the breakout channel that lets you draw with a flick of the wrist, even when pinned.

Ten years later, SouthEDC is still veteran-owned and family-operated — handcrafting every holster in Greensboro, North Carolina. Same workshop. Same standard. No shortcuts.
