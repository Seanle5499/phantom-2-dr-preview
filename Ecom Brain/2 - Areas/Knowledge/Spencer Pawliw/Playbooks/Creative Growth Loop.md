# Creative Growth Loop

## Summary
> Scale creative by reviewing why winners and losers behaved the way they did, then turning that into the next hypothesis.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *Revealing The Only Growth Hack For Facebook Ads*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "you need to review the strategy behind each and every single ad that you create winner or loser... review the executions of your ad... take the learnings and apply them to the next round of creatives"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - Creative Strategy Extraction]]

## Review Checklist
- What was the angle?
- What was the first-frame hook?
- What belief or objection did it address?
- What mechanism did it show?
- Did Meta give it spend?
- Did it convert after getting spend?
- What should the next test keep, remove, or exaggerate?

## SouthEDC Application
Review failed EDC ads for unclear mechanism, weak pocket-use proof, generic tactical claims, or poor first-frame product action.

## Wellness Nest Application
Review failed wellness ads for vague benefits, missing ritual proof, unverified claims, or weak sensory payoff.
