# Native Review Ads

## Summary
> Native ads work because they look like organic proof, not polished advertising.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *The problem with Native ads in 2026 #ecom #motivation #motivational*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "native ads were actually originally... just reviews from real customers... they're camouflage right they don't look like ads and so people bring their guard down... all this is doing is it's just building belief"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - Creative Strategy Extraction]]

## How to Apply
- Use real customer wording.
- Keep layout raw and feed-native.
- Preserve proof; do not over-design.
- Use only truthful reviews with permission where needed.

## SouthEDC Application
Use real customer proof showing pocket fit, daily carry, durability, or gift reaction.

## Wellness Nest Application
Use real customer proof around routine, taste, sleep, digestion, or repeat purchase behavior.
