# Juxtaposition Hooks

## Summary
> Put two ideas together that should not fit. The mismatch creates a curiosity gap.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *How to write hooks that stop 50% of scrolls*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "putting two things that don't go together... toxic and natural Like those don't go together Like how does an allnatural deodorant perform like a toxic one right?... we're doing so much by putting two things that don't go together and doing it creatively"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - Creative Strategy Extraction]]

## Hook Formula
```text
[Unexpected constraint] + [desired outcome]
```

## Examples
- Tiny tool, toolbox-level utility.
- Natural supplement, clinical-strength effect.
- Lightweight carry, survival-grade durability.
