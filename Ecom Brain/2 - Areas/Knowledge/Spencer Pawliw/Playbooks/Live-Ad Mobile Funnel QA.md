# Live-Ad Mobile Funnel QA

## Summary
> Test the real customer path from a live mobile ad, not only theme preview or desktop emulator.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *How We’ll Hit $1M/day on Black Friday (2025)*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "go pull up your phone and QA your entire website right now... especially the day before Black Friday... go to Facebook click one of your ads and go through that customer journey... literally click everything try to break everything you guys don't realize how much shit is broken"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - CRO Shopify Extraction]]

## QA Path
1. Open live ad on phone.
2. Click through to landing page.
3. Check first screen match.
4. Test variants, sizing, and product media.
5. Add to cart.
6. Test payment options.
7. Open policy links.
8. Confirm no broken discounts or shipping surprises.

## SouthEDC Application
QA live tactical product ads for Apple Pay, variant selection, legal/policy links, and shipping thresholds.

## Wellness Nest Application
QA quiz, subscription options, discount code, supplement facts, shipping, and guarantee links.
