# Shopify Health Score

## Summary
> Multiply AOV by CVR. Low score means fix conversion before scaling traffic.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *Why Isn’t My Shopify Store Converting*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "if your Shopify Health score is below 250 you need to start focusing everything onto conversion rate optimization... your Shopify Health score is the number that you get by multiplying your conversion rate by your average order value"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - CRO Shopify Extraction]]

## Formula
```text
Shopify Health Score = AOV x CVR
```

## Use
- Under 250: prioritize CRO.
- Over 300: scaling traffic is more reasonable.

## SouthEDC Application
If AOV is $60 and CVR is 1.5%, score is 90. Fix offer/page first.

## Wellness Nest Application
If AOV is $110 and CVR is 3.5%, score is 385. Traffic scaling is more justified.
