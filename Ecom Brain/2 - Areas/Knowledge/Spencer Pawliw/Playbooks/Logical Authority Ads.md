# Logical Authority Ads

## Summary
> Authority comes from teaching a logical, believable mechanism. Not from pretending to be an expert.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *How to effortlessly create authority in your ecom ads*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "if you provide someone with new information that logically makes sense you are going to establish yourself as an authority... now not only are we speaking in matter of facts versus matter of opinions we are again establishing ourselves... as an authority by again the word choice"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - Creative Strategy Extraction]]

## How to Apply
- Explain the product mechanism clearly.
- Use verified facts only.
- Avoid invented stats or fake expert cues.

## SouthEDC Application
Explain why material, locking mechanism, clip design, or carry profile changes daily usability.

## Wellness Nest Application
Explain the ingredient mechanism, absorption path, timing, or ritual logic with verified support.
