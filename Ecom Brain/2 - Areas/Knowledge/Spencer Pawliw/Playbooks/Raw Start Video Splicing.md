# Raw Start Video Splicing

## Summary
> Small changes to the first seconds can revive a dead video. Test different starting frames before rewriting the whole ad.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *The Instagram Reel Strategy*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "sometimes just cutting a video slightly and changing the starting point has actually been the difference from an ad getting $0 in spend to an ad being able to scale over $2,000 a day a 3second edit... sometimes it's better to work smarter than it is to work harder"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - Creative Strategy Extraction]]

## Workflow
1. Take one raw video.
2. Export three cuts with different first frames.
3. Start each with a different action, payoff, or curiosity moment.
4. Test before reshooting.

## SouthEDC Application
Cut one EDC product video to start with snap, card pull, pocket draw, blade open, or package cut.

## Wellness Nest Application
Cut one wellness product video to start with pour, scoop, fizz, reaction, morning face, or before/after routine.
