# Ad-Comment FAQs

## Summary
> Use real ad comments to build FAQ sections that answer purchase blockers directly on action pages.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *11 CRO Split tests in 17 minutes that generated over $280,000*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "adding FAQs onto the product page... was an 8% increase in profit... if you do not have FAQs on your product page you need to get them in there right away... make sure you actually think about all of the questions... so they can be even more effective"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - CRO Shopify Extraction]]

## Workflow
1. Pull top repeated ad comments.
2. Group into objections.
3. Write short direct answers.
4. Place FAQ below order form or near CTA.

## SouthEDC Application
Turn comments about warranty, legality, compatibility, size, shipping, and durability into FAQ answers.

## Wellness Nest Application
Turn comments about taste, timing, ingredients, fasting, sensitivity, shipping, and guarantee into FAQ answers.
