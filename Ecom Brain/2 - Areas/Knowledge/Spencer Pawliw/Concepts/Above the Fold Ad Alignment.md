# Above the Fold Ad Alignment

## Summary
> If the ad promise and first screen do not match, bounce rate rises. Hero message, visual, and offer must continue the ad.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *Why Do I Have a High Bounce Rate?*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "one of the main reasons why people actually bounce off your store is because they click an ad talking about one thing and then land on your website talking about something completely different so if you actually want to increase your conversion rates you need to make sure that your ads in your above the fold section on your website are fully aligned"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - CRO Shopify Extraction]]

## Checklist
- Same angle.
- Same product visual.
- Same emotional intensity.
- Same offer logic.
- CTA visible on mobile.

## SouthEDC Application
If the ad says "stops pocket scratches," the first screen must show the key organizer solving pocket scratches.

## Wellness Nest Application
If the ad says "bloating relief," the first screen must speak to bloating, not generic wellness.
