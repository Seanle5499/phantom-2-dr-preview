# CRO as Customer Journey

## Summary
> CRO is not button-color tinkering. It is building the page around the ideal customer's purchase questions.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *$6,258,440 in 180 days with CRO - How To ACTUALLY do CRO for DTC Brands*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "conversion re optimization is not just about changing button colors and it's not just about moving sections around okay that definitely can play a part of it but real conversion rate optimization is actually just building a website for your ideal customer so that way they can have a better customer journey"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - CRO Shopify Extraction]]

## How to Apply
- List the buyer's purchase questions.
- Put answers near the decision point.
- Remove design that does not answer a buying concern.

## SouthEDC Application
Answer fit, carry comfort, weight, durability, warranty, and legality before the buyer reaches checkout.

## Wellness Nest Application
Answer ingredients, timing, taste, safety, habit formation, shipping, and guarantee before CTA.
