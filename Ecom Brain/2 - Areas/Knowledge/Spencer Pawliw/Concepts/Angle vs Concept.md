# Angle vs Concept

## Summary
> A concept is the ad structure. An angle is the customer reason to buy. The angle matters more.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *How I use ad angles to hit $100k/days*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "concept is like what we want angles how we choose to sell the product are much more focused on the customer... the angle is more important than the concept because the angle is the reason why the person buys"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - Creative Strategy Extraction]]

## How to Apply
- Start with the buyer's specific reason to care.
- Choose format after the angle is clear.
- Do not call a format test a real creative test.

## SouthEDC Application
Test angles like pocket bulk, fast access, durability, emergency readiness, giftability, and everyday carry comfort before testing formats like listicle, UGC, or comparison.

## Wellness Nest Application
Test angles like sleep quality, bloating relief, morning energy, ritual replacement, and clean ingredients before choosing static, UGC, or founder-led format.
