# Cart-to-Purchase Ratio

## Summary
> Cart-to-purchase ratio diagnoses checkout friction. Healthy stores trend around 3:1; elite stores can reach 2:1.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *What is a Good Shopify Add To Cart Ratio?*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "Best of the Best Brands will have a 2:1 ratio which means for every two ad to carts they get one purchase but on average we see most Shopify brands with a 3:1 or 4:1 add to cart to conversion rate ratio if you have a 6:1 or 7:1 or 10 to1 add to cart ratio that means your ratio is below average and you definitely need to work on optimizing your store"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - CRO Shopify Extraction]]

## How to Apply
- 2:1 = excellent.
- 3:1 = healthy.
- 4:1+ = inspect friction.
- 6:1+ = likely checkout/cart problem.

## SouthEDC Application
Check shipping surprise, variant confusion, compatibility anxiety, legal carry concerns, and trust proof.

## Wellness Nest Application
Check subscription clarity, shipping threshold, taste concerns, guarantee, ingredient concerns, and payment friction.
