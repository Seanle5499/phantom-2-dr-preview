# Visual Copywriting

## Summary
> Good ad copy creates an instant mental image. The buyer should see the benefit before thinking through it.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *Steal My $100k/Day Winning Ad Checklist*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "does the copywriting on this ad create a clear image for the user... cleaner than Mr Clean you automatically probably have an image in your mind of Mr Clean with his bald head and you know the magic clean eraser... that automatically put a visual in your head"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - Creative Strategy Extraction]]

## How to Apply
- Replace abstract claims with concrete sensory images.
- Use comparisons buyers already understand.
- Make the headline drawable.

## SouthEDC Application
Instead of "premium EDC knife", use a visual line like "cuts through thick rope like a fresh razor."

## Wellness Nest Application
Instead of "supports relaxation", use a visual line like "feels like waking up after sleeping in a quiet mountain cabin."
