# Horizontal Product Carousel

## Summary
> On mobile, the product carousel acts like a mini landing page. It should sell horizontally.

## Source Evidence
- Source video: *CRO Strategies From $100M+ Shopify Brands Revealed! (Copy These)*
- Timestamp: not available
- Quote: "make sure your product care sells are as optimized as possible I had been telling clients recently that the goal of your product care cell is almost to build your landing page or product page inside of the Carousel... they shop horizontally they do not shop vertically"
- Extraction: [[2026-07-09 - NotebookLM - CRO Shopify Extraction]]

## Carousel Sequence
1. Hero product image.
2. Size / comparison.
3. Use-case video or GIF.
4. Mechanism / material proof.
5. Review or guarantee.

## SouthEDC Application
Show product, pocket fit, carry demo, material proof, then customer proof.

## Wellness Nest Application
Show product, ingredient proof, use ritual, result expectation, then reviews.
