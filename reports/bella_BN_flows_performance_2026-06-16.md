**Concerns**

- 11 of 34 flows haven't fired yet — normal for post-purchase, but worth confirming triggers are configured correctly.

**Bottom line:** $3,011 lifetime revenue / 30 days / 34 flows = **~$100/flow/month** on average. The 3 abandoned-cart language flows alone drive 43% of her revenue. Highest leverage move is fixing/killing the FR Sunset and adding a real CTA to Customer Thank You + Cross-Sell.
