## 4. Key Findings for Review

### Strengths
1. **Rebuilt email channel from scratch** — filled the gap after MPG ended, tiếp quản 39 agency flows + build 11 flows mới + gửi 18 campaigns in 39 days
2. **Strong growth trajectory** — 0% → 3.4% → 7.6% email revenue share, doubling month-over-month
3. **Better list hygiene** — spam rate 2.5x lower than agency (0.01% vs 0.025%)
4. **Higher AOV** — $131 vs agency's $82, driving more value per conversion
5. **Flow management** — tiếp quản 39 agency flows (16 live, 23 draft) + build 11 flows mới, tổng flow revenue $3,011
6. **Self-managed** — 1 person doing what an agency team did, at lower cost

### Areas to Improve
1. **Click rates** — 0.38% avg, lower than agency (0.48%) and industry benchmark (~2-3%)
2. **3 campaigns with $0 revenue** — content/targeting needs review
3. **Sunset FR flow** — 17K sends with $0 revenue, burning list
4. **Multi-language coverage** — mostly EN, MPG covered EN/DE/FR equally
5. **Campaign frequency** — 0.5/day vs MPG 1.8/day, room to increase cadence
